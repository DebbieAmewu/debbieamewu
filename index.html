I am a PhD Candidate at the University of South Carolina. Before starting my PhD, I completed a Master's degree in Economics at the University of Warwick in the United Kingdom and worked in the Advisory Line of Service at Pricewaterhouse Coopers (PwC), Ghana.

My primary research fields are applied microeconomics, economics of crime, law and economics and public economics. I study how criminal justice policies, law enforcement players and structure impacts economic and public safety outcomes.
